MonthGrid – Calendar Grid Generator for Photo Calendars

With MonthGrid you design the calendar grid for your photo or wall calendar – the layout of weekdays and day numbers – and download all 12 months as transparent PNG at 300 dpi or as SVG. Then place the graphics on your pictures in Canva, InDesign, Affinity, Photoshop or your photo book software.

Design your calendar grid ↑

How it works

  1. Pick the year. Holidays and week numbers are calculated automatically.
  2. Design the cells. Set shape, corners, colors, borders, shadows and fonts for day numbers and weekdays – or start from a template.
  3. Export. Choose PNG or SVG and the resolution, then download all months as a ZIP file.

Features

  • Many cell shapes

    Rectangular, slanted (parallelogram), round, oval, squircle or hexagon – with rounded, beveled or inset corners.

  • Fonts of your choice

    More than 50 included fonts or your own font files. For print, text is converted to vector outlines.

  • Holidays & dates

    Public holidays for Germany (every state), Austria and Switzerland, plus your own entries such as birthdays.

  • Overflow days

    Days from the previous and next month dimmed, as empty boxes or hidden entirely.

  • Print-ready export

    PNG at 150, 300 or 600 dpi with a transparent background, SVG in millimeters – one by one or as a ZIP.

  • Private

    Everything is computed in your browser. Images, fonts and settings are never uploaded.
